# Learning Worker Contract Requirements

## Purpose

Learning Worker extracts candidate evidence.

Learning Worker does not verify claims.

Learning Worker does not approve learning.

Learning Worker does not promote knowledge.

---

## Required Contract Objects

SourceIntakeRecord

CandidateEvidence

ExtractionLineage

WorkerState

OversightExport

---

## Core Principle

All extracted material begins as:

INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE

until independently verified.

---

## Repository Responsibility

Verify intake authenticity.

Extract candidate evidence.

Preserve lineage.

Publish candidate-only exports.
